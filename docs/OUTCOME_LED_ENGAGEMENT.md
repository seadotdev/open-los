# Outcome-Led Engagement Strategy for Open LoS

## The Outcome-Led Promise

Instead of selling features ("we have covenant tracking, deal lifecycle management..."), lead with specific, measurable outcomes that a prospect can hold you accountable to.

---

## Core Outcome Promises

### Promise 1: "Process 3x more loan applications with your current team"
**Proof mechanism:** Baseline their current throughput → implement → measure after 90 days

### Promise 2: "Cut your loan origination software costs by 60-80%"
**Proof mechanism:** Get their current Salesforce/nCino/Mambu invoice → show Open LoS TCO

### Promise 3: "From application to first disbursement in 50% less time"
**Proof mechanism:** Measure their current cycle time → implement → compare

### Promise 4: "Zero covenant breaches missed - ever"
**Proof mechanism:** Real-time monitoring vs. their current quarterly review process

### Promise 5: "Your AI tools work on day one - no integration project"
**Proof mechanism:** Connect Claude/GPT to the API in a live demo

## The Lighthouse Customer Profile

### Ideal Lighthouse Customer Characteristics

| Attribute | Ideal | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Loan volume** | 50-500 originations/year | Small enough to implement fast, large enough to show ROI |
| **Team size** | 5-25 lending professionals | Can't afford enterprise, big enough to feel the pain |
| **Current system** | Excel/Airtable OR expensive legacy LOS | Either desperate for structure OR desperate for cost savings |
| **Tech posture** | Has engineers on staff OR contracts with dev shop | Can deploy, customize, contribute back |
| **AI interest** | Already using Claude/GPT for other tasks | Will immediately see the value of AI-native design |
| **Decision speed** | Can say yes in <30 days | Startup/SMB mentality, not enterprise procurement |
| **Reference willingness** | Will do case study, speak at events | The lighthouse purpose |

### Best Lighthouse Segments (Ranked)

#### Tier 1: Fintech Lenders (Revenue-Based Finance, Invoice Factoring)
**Why they're ideal:**
- Tech-native culture (engineers already on staff)
- Feel the pain of expensive LOS daily
- Move fast, decide fast
- Often frustrated with Mambu/Salesforce pricing
- High transaction volume = visible ROI quickly

**Example companies:** Clearco-style revenue lenders, invoice factoring startups, merchant cash advance fintechs

**Outreach angle:** "Your LOS costs more than your cloud infrastructure. That's backwards."

#### Tier 2: Credit Unions (Progressive, Tech-Forward)
**Why they're ideal:**
- Cooperative model = willing to share success stories
- Regulatory burden makes audit trail compelling
- Often stuck on ancient core systems
- Community-minded (open source resonates philosophically)
- Budget-conscious (60-80% savings is meaningful)

**Example targets:** Credit unions with "innovation" or "digital" in recent press releases, members of CU tech consortiums

**Outreach angle:** "Your members deserve modern lending. Your budget demands open source."

#### Tier 3: Neobanks / Challenger Banks
**Why they're ideal:**
- Building from scratch (no migration complexity)
- Tech-first culture
- Want to move faster than incumbents
- Data sovereignty matters (regulatory pressure)
- Often venture-backed (looking for efficiency)

**Outreach angle:** "Your competitors are locked into Salesforce roadmaps. You don't have to be."

#### Tier 4: Direct Lending Platforms / Alternative Lenders
**Why they're ideal:**
- High volume, thin margins (cost savings matter)
- Often serving underserved markets
- Need efficiency to be viable
- Tech-forward out of necessity

**Outreach angle:** "When your margin is 3%, you can't afford $100K/year in software licensing."

## Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1: Land One Lighthouse (Weeks 1-8)

**Goal:** One paying customer with a signed case study agreement

**Actions:**
1. **Identify 20 ideal prospects** from Tier 1-2 segments
2. **Personalized outreach** (not spray-and-pray):
   - Research their current stack (job postings reveal tech)
   - Find their pain (Glassdoor reviews, G2 complaints about competitors)
   - Reference their specific situation in outreach
3. **Offer a "Proof of Outcome" pilot:**
   - We implement Open LoS alongside your current system
   - Run parallel for 30-60 days
   - Measure the three outcomes (throughput, cost, cycle time)
   - Only pay if outcomes are achieved
4. **Close with case study agreement** as part of the deal

**Channels:**
- LinkedIn (direct to Head of Lending, VP Credit, CTO)
- Warm intros via fintech investors
- Fintech Slack communities
- Conference networking (LendIt, Money20/20)

### Phase 2: Build Social Proof (Weeks 8-16)

**Goal:** 3-5 customers + published case study + community activity

**Actions:**
1. **Publish lighthouse case study** with real numbers:
   - "How [COMPANY] cut LOS costs by 70% and increased throughput 2.5x"
   - Get permission for logo usage
2. **Content marketing:**
   - "The True Cost of Loan Origination Software" (calculator)
   - "Why AI Agents Can't Work with Your Current LOS" (technical deep-dive)
   - "Open Source in Financial Services: A Compliance Guide"
3. **Community building:**
   - GitHub stars campaign (technical credibility)
   - Discord/Slack community for users
   - Contribute to fintech podcasts
4. **Reference selling:**
   - Every call includes "want to talk to a current customer?"

### Phase 3: Scale Acquisition (Weeks 16+)

**Goal:** Repeatable acquisition engine

**Actions:**
1. **Outbound at scale:**
   - SDR team with outcome-led scripts
   - Account-based marketing to target list
2. **Inbound engine:**
   - SEO for "Salesforce alternative for lending" etc.
   - Paid search for high-intent keywords
   - Integration marketplace listings
3. **Partner channel:**
   - Implementation partners (consulting firms)
   - Complementary vendors (banking core providers)
   - Fintech accelerators (deal flow)
4. **Product-led growth:**
   - Free tier for <$10M portfolio
   - Self-serve implementation guide
   - Community support tier

## Finding Lighthouse Customers: Tactical Playbook

### Signal Hunting: How to Find Ready Buyers

**Signal 1: Job postings**
- Search for: "loan origination" + "Salesforce" or "nCino" + (any frustration signals)
- Companies hiring for "lending operations analyst" = growing, need efficiency
- Companies hiring for "LOS administrator" = have system, might want to switch

**Signal 2: Tech stack data**
- BuiltWith, Wappalyzer for web tech
- G2/Gartner reviews for their current LOS (find frustrated users)
- Crunchbase for recent funding (money to spend on infrastructure)

**Signal 3: Public statements**
- Press releases about "digital transformation"
- Executives speaking at fintech conferences
- LinkedIn posts complaining about vendor lock-in

**Signal 4: Regulatory triggers**
- Companies expanding to EU (GDPR = data sovereignty concerns)
- Recent exam findings (need better audit trails)
- Compliance hires (investing in infrastructure)

**Signal 5: Community activity**
- Active in fintech Slack groups
- GitHub users from the company
- Open source contributions elsewhere (cultural fit)

### Outreach Sequence (7-touch)

| Day | Channel | Message Focus |
|-----|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Email | Outcome promise + question |
| 3 | LinkedIn | Connect request with context |
| 5 | Email | Case study / social proof |
| 8 | LinkedIn | Value-add content share |
| 12 | Email | "Am I wrong about [their pain]?" |
| 15 | Phone | Brief voicemail referencing emails |
| 20 | Email | Breakup email ("Should I close your file?") |

### Qualification Questions

Before investing in a prospect, confirm:

1. **Pain confirmed:** "What's your biggest frustration with your current loan origination process?"
2. **Budget authority:** "If this solves the problem, who else needs to approve?"
3. **Timeline:** "When do you need this working by?"
4. **Technical fit:** "Do you have engineers who could deploy open-source software, or would you need implementation help?"
5. **Outcome alignment:** "If we could [specific outcome], would that justify the switch?"

## The Outcome-Led Demo

Don't demo features. Demo outcomes.

### Demo Script (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-5: Their situation**
- "Walk me through a loan application from when it hits your desk to disbursement"
- "What breaks? Where do things get stuck?"
- "How much time does your team spend on data entry vs. analysis?"

**Minutes 5-15: The outcome in action**
- Show a deal flowing through the system
- Show AI agent processing documents (live)
- Show covenant monitoring catching a breach in real-time
- Show the audit trail an examiner would see

**Minutes 15-20: The proof**
- "Here's the case study from [LIGHTHOUSE CUSTOMER]"
- "They saw X% improvement in Y"
- "Here's the code - MIT licensed, your engineers can audit it"

**Minutes 20-25: The economics**
- "What do you pay for [current system] today?"
- Calculator showing TCO comparison
- "The software is free. Implementation is [X]. Support is [Y]."

**Minutes 25-30: Next steps**
- "Want to run a parallel pilot?"
- "Let's define the three metrics we'll measure"
- "I'll send a summary with the outcome guarantee in writing"

## Pricing That Supports Outcome-Led Sales

### Model: Outcome-Based Pricing

**Option A: Pay for Performance**
- Implementation fee: $X
- Outcome guarantee: If you don't see [specific outcome] in 90 days, we refund 50%
- Ongoing: Support subscription (optional)

**Option B: Shared Savings**
- Calculate their current LOS cost
- Implementation fee: % of first-year savings
- If they save $200K/year, you charge $40K implementation

**Option C: Traditional + Guarantee**
- Fixed implementation fee
- Fixed support subscription
- "Satisfaction guarantee" with clear outcome metrics

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Open source isn't enterprise-ready" | "Linux runs the banks. PostgreSQL holds the data. Open source IS enterprise infrastructure. And here's our conformance test suite - 161 tests proving correctness." |
| "We don't have engineers to maintain it" | "That's why we offer managed support. Or we can connect you with implementation partners. You get the benefits of open source without needing a team." |
| "Migration is too risky" | "That's why we run parallel pilots. Your existing system stays live. We prove the outcomes before you switch." |
| "Our compliance team won't approve" | "Let's bring them in. Immutable audit trail, deterministic calculations, full source code review. What specific compliance concern can I address?" |
| "What if you disappear?" | "The code is MIT licensed. You can fork it, hire anyone to maintain it, or keep running it forever. That's the whole point - you can't be abandoned." |
