# The Open LOS Manifesto

## The Walls Are Going Up

Salesforce is cutting off API access. Slack is blocking AI agents. The enterprise software giants can smell what's coming, and they're terrified.

They should be.

For decades, these companies have extracted rent from businesses by controlling your data. They built walled gardens and called them "ecosystems." They created lock-in and called it "integration." They charged you to access your own information and called it a "platform fee."

Now AI threatens to make their moats obsolete. An AI agent with proper API access can do in seconds what their bloated UIs do in hours. So what do they do? They don't compete on product quality. They don't build better AI themselves. They slam the gates shut and hope you don't notice.

This is the death rattle of the old software regime.

## The Cost of Captivity

When you build on someone else's platform, you inherit their constraints and their conflicts of interest. When Salesforce decides their mediocre AI assistant is the only one allowed to touch your data, that's your constraint now. When they raise prices because they've locked you in, you pay.

Worse: when they start dying—and they are dying—they will squeeze harder. They will make increasingly desperate moves to protect their revenue. Every "policy update" will be another turn of the screw.

The enterprises that built on these platforms are now held hostage. Their data is trapped. Their workflows are frozen. Their ability to innovate is bounded by what the platform vendor allows.

This is not a technology problem. It's a sovereignty problem.

## A New Dawn

There has never been a better time to own your own system of record.

AI can now write, test, and maintain software at a level that was impossible two years ago. The cost of building quality systems has collapsed. The only reason to pay for overpriced enterprise software today is inertia and fear.

We reject both.

**Open LOS** is what happens when you ask a simple question: *What would banking software look like if it were built for the AI era, not grudgingly retrofitted for it?*

The answer:

- **AI as a first-class citizen**, not an afterthought bolted on to justify a price increase. Humans and AI agents call the same APIs, get the same audit trails, and have the same capabilities.

- **Headless by design**. No UI opinions forced on you. Build your own frontend. Use AI to generate one. Or don't use a frontend at all—let agents drive the system directly.

- **Deterministic where it matters**. Financial calculations, covenant tests, stage transitions—these are not "AI features." They are mathematical operations that must be correct and auditable. The AI explains; the system computes.

- **Immutable audit trails**. Every mutation, every decision, every AI session logged permanently. Not for compliance theater—for truth. When a regulator asks "why did this loan get approved?", there's an answer.

- **Radically open**. MIT licensed. Full source. Your data stays yours. Fork it, modify it, deploy it on your infrastructure. No API policies will ever lock you out of your own system.

## Who This Is For

This is not for JPMorgan. They have a thousand developers and can build anything.

This is for the next JPMorgan.

It's for the fintech that needs loan origination but can't afford Salesforce's enterprise pricing. It's for the credit union that wants modern software but has a small IT team. It's for the startup that will become a bank but needs to move fast today.

It's for everyone who has been told "you need enterprise software to do serious lending" and suspected, correctly, that this was a lie.

The marginal customer—too small for incumbents to care about but too ambitious to stay small—is who we serve.

## The Bet We're Making

We believe:

1. **AI-maintained software will outcompete human-maintained software on cost and quality.** Open LOS is continuously tested, documented, and improved by AI. This is not a one-time build; it's an ongoing collaboration between human direction and AI execution.

2. **Open source will win in infrastructure.** The same pattern that replaced proprietary databases with Postgres, proprietary operating systems with Linux, and proprietary cloud APIs with Kubernetes will play out in business software. The future of lending infrastructure is open.

3. **Data sovereignty is a competitive advantage.** The companies that own their systems outright—that can switch AI providers at will, that can integrate with anything, that face no artificial barriers to innovation—will outperform those trapped in vendor ecosystems.

4. **The next generation of financial institutions will be AI-native.** They won't have legacy systems to retrofit. They won't have decades of technical debt. They will start with systems designed for human-AI collaboration and will move faster than anything before them.

## What We've Built

Open LOS is a complete B2B lending platform:

- **Full deal lifecycle**: Broker intake, origination, underwriting, closing, monitoring
- **Entity management**: Companies, people, ownership graphs, guarantor relationships
- **Financial analysis**: Spreading, ratio computation, deterministic and auditable
- **Covenant tracking**: Definition, testing, grace periods, waivers, alerts
- **Loan ledger**: Facilities, accounts, disbursements, repayments, arrears tracking
- **Bank transaction monitoring**: Ingestion, categorization, liquidity analysis
- **Approval workflows**: Stage transitions, facility approvals, covenant waivers
- **Complete audit trail**: Every mutation logged with actor, timestamp, and context

All accessible via API. All tested by 161 conformance tests. All designed for AI agents to operate as easily as humans.

Open LOS is not just software. It's a declaration of independence from the dying software empires.
