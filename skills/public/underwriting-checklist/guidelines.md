# Underwriting Guidelines

## Borrower Assessment

### Entity Verification

| Check | Standard | Exception Process |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Entity registration | Active, in good standing | Document reason if recently formed |
| Operating history | Minimum 2 years | Startup exception requires additional equity |
| Management experience | 5+ years in industry | Document compensating factors |

### Ownership Structure

- Document all beneficial owners with 25%+ ownership
- Trace ownership chain to ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs)
- Flag complex offshore structures for enhanced review
- Verify no sanctioned parties in ownership chain

### Background Checks

Required for:
- All guarantors
- Officers with signing authority
- Beneficial owners with 25%+ ownership

Review:
- Credit reports (minimum 650 FICO for personal guarantees)
- Criminal background (felony disqualification period: 7 years)
- Bankruptcy history (minimum 3 years since discharge)
- OFAC/sanctions screening

## Financial Analysis

### Ratio Thresholds

| Ratio | Minimum | Target | Automatic Decline |
|-------|---------|--------|-------------------|
| DSCR | 1.15x | 1.25x+ | Below 1.0x |
| Leverage (Debt/Equity) | 3.0x max | Under 2.0x | Above 4.0x |
| Current Ratio | 1.0x | 1.5x+ | Below 0.8x |
| Interest Coverage | 2.0x | 3.0x+ | Below 1.5x |

### Financial Statement Requirements

| Loan Amount | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| Under $500K | Tax returns + internal statements |
| $500K - $2M | CPA-reviewed statements |
| $2M - $5M | CPA-audited statements |
| Above $5M | Big 4 audit preferred |

### Trend Analysis

Flag for additional review:
- Revenue decline > 10% YoY
- Gross margin decline > 5 points
- Working capital deterioration
- Increasing days receivable (>15 day increase)
- Inventory buildup (>30 day increase in days inventory)

## Cash Flow Analysis

### Projection Standards

- Base case: Management projections with documented assumptions
- Downside case: -20% revenue, +10% costs
- Severe downside: -35% revenue, +15% costs

### DSCR Requirements by Scenario

| Scenario | Required DSCR |
|----------|---------------|
| Base case | 1.25x minimum |
| Downside | 1.10x minimum |
| Severe downside | 1.00x minimum |

## Collateral Guidelines

### LTV Limits by Collateral Type

| Collateral | Maximum LTV | Advance Rate |
|------------|-------------|--------------|
| Commercial real estate | 75% | 65-70% |
| Equipment | 80% | 50-70% |
| Accounts receivable | 85% | 70-80% |
| Inventory (raw) | 50% | 30-40% |
| Inventory (finished) | 65% | 40-55% |

### Appraisal Requirements

| Loan Amount | Real Estate | Equipment |
|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| Under $500K | Desktop/BPO | Dealer quote |
| $500K - $2M | Drive-by | Desktop appraisal |
| Above $2M | Full MAI appraisal | Full appraisal |

### Insurance Requirements

- Property coverage: 100% replacement cost
- Liability coverage: $1M minimum
- Lender named as loss payee and additional insured
- 30-day cancellation notice requirement
