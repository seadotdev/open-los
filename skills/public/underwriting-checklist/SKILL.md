# Underwriting Checklist

## Purpose

This skill guides thorough due diligence for commercial loan underwriting. Use this checklist when evaluating a new deal or reviewing an existing application for credit approval.

## Steps

### 1. Borrower Assessment

- [ ] Verify legal entity status and registration
- [ ] Review corporate structure and ownership chain
- [ ] Check management experience and track record
- [ ] Verify key person backgrounds
- [ ] Review litigation history and pending cases
- [ ] Check for regulatory compliance issues

See [guidelines.md](guidelines.md#borrower-assessment) for detailed criteria.

### 2. Financial Analysis

- [ ] Spread last 3 years of financial statements
- [ ] Calculate key ratios:
  - Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
  - Leverage ratio (Debt/Equity)
  - Current ratio
  - Quick ratio
  - Interest coverage ratio
- [ ] Identify trends and anomalies
- [ ] Compare to industry benchmarks
- [ ] Verify financial statement quality (audited vs. reviewed vs. compiled)
- [ ] Review management accounts if available

See [guidelines.md](guidelines.md#financial-analysis) for ratio thresholds.

### 3. Cash Flow Analysis

- [ ] Analyze historical cash flow patterns
- [ ] Project future cash flows based on business plan
- [ ] Stress test under adverse scenarios:
  - Revenue decline of 20%
  - Cost increase of 15%
  - Combined adverse scenario
- [ ] Verify cash flow sources are sustainable
- [ ] Identify seasonal patterns or concentration risks

### 4. Collateral Review

- [ ] Obtain independent asset valuations
- [ ] Conduct lien searches (UCC, tax liens, judgments)
- [ ] Verify insurance coverage adequacy
- [ ] Assess collateral maintenance requirements
- [ ] Calculate loan-to-value (LTV) ratio
- [ ] Evaluate collateral liquidity and marketability

### 5. Industry & Market Analysis

- [ ] Research industry outlook and trends
- [ ] Assess competitive positioning
- [ ] Identify customer concentration risks
- [ ] Review supplier dependencies
- [ ] Evaluate regulatory environment
- [ ] Consider macroeconomic factors

### 6. Transaction Structure

- [ ] Evaluate proposed loan amount vs. need
- [ ] Assess term and amortization appropriateness
- [ ] Review proposed covenants
- [ ] Identify required conditions precedent
- [ ] Consider guarantor requirements
- [ ] Evaluate pricing relative to risk

## Red Flags

The following issues require immediate escalation:

- Financial statements show material inconsistencies
- Borrower has undisclosed related party transactions
- Significant customer or supplier concentration (>25%)
- Declining revenue trend for 2+ consecutive periods
- DSCR below 1.0x on historical basis
- Unresolved litigation exceeding 10% of net worth
- Management unwilling to provide requested information
- Collateral valuation concerns

See [red-flags.md](red-flags.md) for the complete list and escalation procedures.

## Output

Generate a structured underwriting memo covering:

1. **Executive Summary** - Key findings and recommendation
2. **Borrower Overview** - Entity structure, management, history
3. **Financial Analysis** - Spreads, ratios, trends
4. **Risk Assessment** - Identified risks and mitigants
5. **Collateral Summary** - Coverage and quality
6. **Recommendation** - Approve/decline with conditions
